## Support Services (10 service departments)

Each led by a **Director**. These are the non-academic operational divisions.

### 1. Finance and Control (FC)
- **Director:** *(listed on directors page)*
- Financial and economic service provision.

### 2. Education and Student Affairs (ESA)
- Education support service for students and teaching.

### 3. Research Support & Valorization (RSV)
- Two units: Research Support Office (RSO) and startup helpdesk **The Gate**.

### 4. Equipment and Prototype Center (EPC)
- Technical research support for TU/e, external institutes, and industry.

### 5. Campus & Facilities (C&F)
- Development and management of the 75-hectare TU/e Science Park.

### 6. Library and Information Services (LIS)
- IT, library services, cybersecurity, privacy, education systems, data management, and platforms.

### 7. Human Resources Management (HRM)
- Staff environment, development, and HR policies.

### 8. Communication Expertise Center (CEC)
- TU/e communication policy and external communications.

### 9. General Affairs (GA)
- Board secretariat, Board staff, and entities involved in CvB matters.

### 10. CASIMIR
- Listed as a separate unit on the organogram (bridging chips and high-tech systems).

---

## Research Institutes (4 institutes)

Cross-departmental research organizations.

### 1. Institute for Complex Molecular Systems (ICMS)
- Central hub for molecular systems research.

### 2. Eindhoven Institute for Renewable Energy Systems (EIRES)
- Accelerating the energy transition.

### 3. Eindhoven Artificial Intelligence Systems Institute (EAISI)
- Central hub for AI research at TU/e.

### 4. Casimir Institute
- Bridging future chips and high-tech systems through deep integration of design, equipment, materials, processes, and foundational science.
